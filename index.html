The idea

Most families are
sitting on the answer.

Equity in the house. A 401k. A brokerage account. A policy with cash value. Four places most people never count when they decide they can't help.

Moving it takes four professions at once. A lender who knows what underwriting accepts, a CPA who knows what is taxed and what is merely reported, an attorney who knows what a deed will do to your property taxes, and a broker who can find the house.

Get one right and the other three wrong and a family ends up with a reassessment, a tax bill, or a document nobody can undo.
